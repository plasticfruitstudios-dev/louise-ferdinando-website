Vintage Jewels

My goal is to infuse modern clothes with a quirky twist rendered by wearing them with vintage accessories. Some pieces you will find here are actually antique and can lend themselves to home interiors as well as wearing. Some will just speak to you and you will want to wear them with everything. Yet others seem impossibly glamorous and because of their gentle patina, can be worn in a casual manner. One thing they all do is strengthen your look, adding individuality in an increasingly homogeneous world.

Press

My jewellery has been featured in magazines such as Dazed and Confused, Metal Magazine & Mr Porter, along with films such as An Education, The Man From Uncle & The boat that rocked. Follow my Instagram for stills and inspiration!

About Me

Using my background in luxury retail (Butler and Wilson and Valentino) and prop houses, I have over many years built up a vast collection of fashion accessories and jewellery which I have made available to costume designers, stylists and finally began selling to the public 18 years ago. Please contact me for information on current stock for hire or sale.
